Able250 Essay Contest

Celebrating America's 250th Anniversary

A Declaration of Interdependence

We invite you to share how Americans with disabilities have shaped the founding ideals of our nation — and how those contributions have helped "form a more perfect Union" since 1776.

Submit Your Essay
Theme

The American Story from 1776 Onward

We invite essays that highlight the essential role of Americans with disabilities from 1776 onward, including patriots wounded in the Revolutionary War who continued to serve the new republic despite their injuries.

Submissions should show how individuals with disabilities have been part of the American story from the very beginning — not as exceptions, but as core contributors to liberty, justice, and the ongoing pursuit of a more perfect union.

Contest Overview

Guidelines

  • 500–800 words (blog-style essay)
  • Original, unpublished work with or without AI assistance
  • Open to U.S. residents age 16 and older
  • Submit using the form below

Why Enter

Who Can Enter

Eligibility

  • U.S. residents only, age 16 and older
  • Employees and volunteers of Able250 and their immediate families are ineligible
  • Co-authored entries are not accepted
Format & Rights

Submission Requirements

  • 500–800 words
  • Must address the theme directly
  • Include title and word count
  • By submitting, you grant Able250 non-exclusive rights to edit, publish, and promote the essay on AbleAngel.org and additional media, with credit to you
  • Major edits will be shared with contributors before publication
How Essays Are Scored

Judging Criteria

100 points total.

  • Historical accuracy and relevance to the theme
  • Insight into disability contributions to "a more perfect union"
  • Clarity, originality, and writing quality
  • Strength of examples

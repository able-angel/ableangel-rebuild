About Us

Our Mission

Mission

ABLE ANGEL will provide advocacy, education, marketing and related expertise to individuals, grantmakers, corporations and other institutions, to have a positive impact on the lives of people with disabilities.

Our Vision

Vision

Individuals with disabilities are thriving and fully engaged with their families and in their communities.


The systems that support people with disabilities will be fully resourced to be effective, impactful, and person-centered.

Our Values

What We Believe

A positive supportive environment, based on the interchange between and among all people, offers the greatest opportunity for the maximization of human potential.

Our response to those in need is both a gift and a mandate, which we carry out in affiliation with the wider community.

Our present efforts shall be a link between those who have been supported in the past, present and those who will require support in the future.

The value of risk in service to people, and seek to explore new, improved ways to support that service.

The barriers between and among people are creations of human nature, and we shall advocate for the needs of those we support and for the elimination of labels that separate humankind.

The population of people with disabilities are best supported by creating opportunities that promote self-sufficiency to the greatest extent possible.

The importance of cooperation between the for-profit, government and nonprofit sectors of society on behalf of those we support.

Our Impact

Impact

ABLE ANGEL will have positive impact on the lives of people with disabilities through its advocacy, sponsorship, recruitment and educational strategies. This impact will be multiplied by enlisting the support and acting as conduit for individuals, granting organizations, corporate sponsors, agencies and institutions with similar intent.

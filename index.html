The ABLE Act: Opportunity for Financial Independence

The ABLE Act creates a powerful opportunity for people with disabilities to save and invest without jeopardizing their essential benefits. Through education and awareness, Able Angel helps individuals, families, and organizations understand and take advantage of this opportunity.

With the 2026 expansion, the opportunity extends to millions more Americans — veterans, people with adult-onset conditions, and families nationwide. Opportunity brings ability to life.
